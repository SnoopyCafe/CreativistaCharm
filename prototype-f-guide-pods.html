The name

creativista

noun. Our own word, and yes, it is meant to make you smile.

A person who gives birth to creativity: an innovator, a designer, a promoter, a follower of the latest creative ideas.

It borrows the Spanish suffix -ista, the one that marks a person by what they do, the same way fashionista does. It is also exactly what we are hired to grow in a room full of eight-year-olds.

Tia S. Miller

Founder and owner, Creativista Charm LLC.

Tia is the person a parks director calls, the person who hires the instructors, and the face of the work in the coverage listed above. Her media kit carries the full background, speaking topics, and photography.
