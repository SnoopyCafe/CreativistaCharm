The name

Creativista

A person who gives birth to creativity: an innovator, a designer, a promoter, a follower of the latest creative ideas.

The word is ours. It borrows the Spanish suffix -ista, the one that marks a person by what they do, the same way fashionista does. Yes, that is meant to make you smile. It is also exactly what we are hired to grow in a room full of eight-year-olds.
