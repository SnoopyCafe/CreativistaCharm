Creativista Charm — archived design directions

These are archived design directions retained for reference. View the live site.

A · Felt & Floss

Camp pennant and patch culture. Nineteen agencies as earned, embroidered patches on deep felt; programs hang as pennants from a cord.

Type
Graduate, Grand Hotel, Archivo
Ground
Dark felt teal
Reads
Warm, earned, tactile

B · The Program Guide

The seasonal catalog a recreation department already publishes. Thumb tabs down the edge, dense listings, a printed partner index.

Type
Saira Condensed, Public Sans
Ground
White stock, four seasonal fields
Reads
Official, scannable, familiar

C · Four-Color Pull

A screenprinted community poster. A ray burst turning behind drawn lettering, halftoned photographs, and the billing crawling along the border.

Type
Bungee, Barlow Condensed, Barlow
Ground
Uncoated sheet, four flat inks
Reads
Loud, civic, energetic

D · Sister Brand

Built on the real CreativistaPods / Creativista Learning design system: its teal and amber tokens, Gabarito type, pill buttons, and the dashed orbit device.

Type
Gabarito, Schibsted Grotesk
Ground
White, amber wash, lilac band
Reads
Consistent with the sister site

E · Poster, Pods Materials

F · Guide, Pods Materials

B's catalog rebuilt in the sister brand's system. Thumb tabs, the dense listings table, and the dotted-leader index survive; the flat seasonal fields do not.

Type
Gabarito, Schibsted Grotesk
Ground

All content is real. Nineteen clients, eight press outlets, both regional phone numbers, three open roles, and the founder bio all come from the current site. No statistics, testimonials, years-in-business, or student counts were invented, because none were available to use.

Two things to check before launch. Three of the photographs are AdobeStock licensed to the original site — confirm the license carries over. Press outlets are set as text rather than reproduced logos.
