FAQ

Questions agencies ask first.

What ages do you work with?

Grades PK through 8th across programs and tutoring.

Which areas do you serve?

Greater Hartford, Connecticut and South Florida. Each region has its own phone line, and instructors are placed locally.

Do we have to provide staff?

No. Recruiting, staffing, and on-site delivery are part of what we do, along with the curriculum and materials.

Can a program run at our own site?

Yes. After-school programming is delivered at your location, sized to the room and the roster you already have.

What is a learning pod, and is that you?

Learning pods run under Creativista Learning at CreativistaPods.com, our sister program. Different brand, same people. For a pod, start there.

How do we start?

Email info@CreativistaCharm.com or call the line for your region. It goes to Tia directly.
